Welcome to tailmon

tailmon shows the CPU, GPU, RAM, IO, disk and temperature of the machines on your Tailscale tailnet, and finds, searches and opens files on all of them at once. Nothing is installed on the machines it watches.

This installer adds:

  • the tailmon command, in /usr/local/bin;
  • its manual page (man tailmon);
  • optionally, the tailscale command, if the Tailscale app is installed without it.

You will also need:

  • Tailscale, signed in to your tailnet;
  • SSH access by key (no password prompt) to the machines you want to watch;
  • Python 3. If it is missing, macOS offers to install the Command Line Tools after this installation.
